Domino Ayisyen (Haitian Domino) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Domino Ayisyen ("the game", "we") is a Haitian-style domino game, in Kreyòl and English, by Jocelyn Emilus. This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: we collect nothing.

No account, no collection

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect, receive, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

To work, the game remembers a few things only on your own phone, tablet or computer:

  • your settings (language, sound, tile hints),
  • the names you type for the players,
  • your avatar and the country flag you choose,
  • your progress (your wins and the places you have reached),
  • your stats (matches, rounds, dominos and dekabès),
  • the game in progress and the last rounds (kept for 24 hours).

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game, or clearing the site data of the web version, erases it. The Profile screen has a button to erase your stats.

Internet use

The game in the App Store contains everything it needs and works with no internet. It makes no web requests. The web version at domino.newohq.com is served by GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, which, like any web server, may keep ordinary technical logs such as your internet address. We do not use that information.

In Settings, the photo credits list the photographers. If you tap a photographer's link, your browser opens unsplash.com, which follows its own privacy policy.

Advertising, tracking and analytics

The game has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it uses none of the advertising or tracking features of your device. There are no purchases in the game.

Children

The game does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
