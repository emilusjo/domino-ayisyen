Common questions

How do I play?

Tap Learn to play on the start screen for a short lesson with real tiles. The full rules are in Settings → How to play. In short: play a tile that matches a number at one end of the table. Play your last tile (m’tonbe) to win the round. A tile that fits both different ends is a dekabès and counts 2. The first player or team to win 4 rounds in a row wins the match (a Vièj).

Do I need an account or internet?

No. There are no accounts, and the game works with no internet.

The game forgot my progress

Your progress is saved only on your own device. It is lost if you delete the game, reset your phone, or (in the web version) clear the site data. There is no online copy.

Can I change the language or the player names?

Yes. Choose Kreyòl or English on the start screen or in Settings. Settings → Player names lets you type your own names. Settings → Profile lets you pick an avatar and a country flag.

Where are the photos from?

Settings → Photo credits lists the photographers. Most photos come from photographers on Unsplash. Some pictures are made with AI and are marked as such in the credits, and some places are drawn in code.

I found a mistake in the Kreyòl

Please tell us. We want the texts to be right, and we welcome corrections from native speakers.
